v1 screens and navigation

A rough, clickable phone for Screens and navigation for v1. Same app and same songs in each layout. D is the mix you described; A to C are the first three options. Flip with the arrows below.

D · Three tabs (your mix)

A small bar at the bottom with Queues, Player and Library, all in thumb range. The Library has tabs at the top for folders, albums, artists and songs. On the Queues and Library tabs, a slim strip above the bar shows what's playing; tap it for the Player.

A · Two tabs

A bottom bar with Library and Queues. A mini-player sits above it and opens the now-playing screen. The Library has its own tabs for songs, albums, artists and folders. Closest to most Android music apps.

B · Swipe tabs

One row of tabs you can swipe through: Queues, Folders, Albums, Artists, Songs. The Queues tab shows one Queue at a time, with chips to switch. Light and dense, in the spirit of Musicolet.

C · Player first

Opens on the song that's playing, with its Queue underneath. Queues slide in from the left, and the Library opens from the search button. Fewest screens.

Things to try

  • Play a song from an album. A new Queue appears, and the old one stays.
  • Long-press (or right-click) a song in a Queue to move or copy it. Drag the handle to reorder.
  • Open lyrics on the now-playing screen. It asks for folder access once.
  • Road trip has two Missing songs.
  • In Settings, remove Music/ and see your Queues keep their songs as Missing.
